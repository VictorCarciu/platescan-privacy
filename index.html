Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

1. Who operates PlateScan

PlateScan is operated by Victor Carciu, an individual based in Romania, European Union ("PlateScan", "I", "me", or "my"). PlateScan does not require a user account.

Privacy questions or requests: carcium@gmail.com.

2. Information PlateScan processes

Meal photos and descriptions

When you ask PlateScan to analyze a meal, it processes the photo you submit or the meal description you type. It also processes the resulting food, portion, calorie, and nutrient estimates. Food names and meal context may be processed when you request food replacements or ideas for later in the day.

Meal records and calorie targets

Saved meals may include food names, estimated portions, calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, meal source, and timestamps. PlateScan also stores your daily calorie target and target history. Information entered into the guided target calculator, such as sex, age, height, weight, activity level, goal, and desired pace, is used on your device and is not intentionally sent to PlateScan's backend.

Optional analytics

PlateScan asks for permission before enabling Firebase Analytics. If you choose Not now, Analytics remains disabled and the app's core features remain available. You can later allow or withdraw permission from the Analytics preference on Home.

After you opt in, Analytics may process app and device identifiers, screen views, and product interactions such as success or failure, error category, food count, broad calorie range, replacement type, progress range, or meal-edit type. PlateScan's custom Analytics events do not include meal photos, food names, meal descriptions, or exact calorie or nutrient values.

Backend diagnostics

The backend processes technical information needed to provide and protect the service. This may include IP and network information, a request identifier, endpoint, HTTP status, duration, image byte size, food count, AI provider and model, and error category. Its structured diagnostics are designed not to include meal descriptions, food names, image contents, raw AI responses, authorization headers, or API keys.

3. How information is used

  • To identify foods and estimate portions, calories, and nutrients at your request.
  • To provide food replacement and rest-of-day suggestions.
  • To save and display meal history, targets, and progress on your device.
  • To operate and secure the service, diagnose errors, enforce limits, and prevent abuse.
  • If you consent, to understand app usage and improve PlateScan.

4. AI processing and nutrition estimates

Meal photos and descriptions are sent over the internet to PlateScan's backend hosted by Render. The backend sends relevant photo or text content and meal context to the OpenAI API to generate food identification, portion, calorie, nutrient, replacement, or suggestion results.

AI results are estimates and may be incomplete or inaccurate. Review foods, portions, calories, and nutrients before saving or relying on them. PlateScan is not a medical device and does not provide diagnoses, treatment, or professional medical or nutritional advice.

5. Local storage and backups

Meal history, nutrition records, calorie targets and history, disclosure acknowledgments, Analytics preference, and daily suggestion state are stored locally. PlateScan has no account-based cloud synchronization or server-side meal-history database. Your operating system or backup service may include app data in device backups, depending on your settings; PlateScan does not control those backups.

6. Service providers

  • OpenAI processes AI inputs and outputs used by PlateScan.
  • Render hosts the backend and may process network, request, and infrastructure information.
  • Google Firebase Analytics processes optional usage events and app/device identifiers only after you opt in through PlateScan.
  • Expo/EAS provides app build and update-delivery infrastructure and may process technical build, update, request, and device metadata. PlateScan does not intentionally send meal photos to Expo/EAS for runtime meal analysis.
  • Apple distributes the iOS app and provides platform services under its own privacy terms.

These providers may process information under their own terms, privacy notices, and account configurations.

7. Retention and deletion

PlateScan does not intentionally save meal photos, descriptions, or AI responses in its own backend database. Uploaded images are held in backend memory while a request is processed. Technical logs may be retained by hosting infrastructure and service providers.

OpenAI may retain API inputs and outputs under the terms, policies, and account settings applicable to PlateScan. Render may retain application or infrastructure logs under its service settings. Firebase Analytics retains data under the Google Analytics property configuration. PlateScan does not promise that third-party copies or logs are deleted immediately.

Deleting a meal removes it from local app storage. Uninstalling normally removes local app data, although backups may retain or restore a copy. Neither action automatically deletes Analytics records, infrastructure logs, backups, or information previously processed by a provider.

Withdrawing Analytics consent disables future Analytics collection by PlateScan and resets locally held Analytics data where supported. It does not automatically delete records previously received by Google. Contact carcium@gmail.com with a privacy or deletion request. Because PlateScan has no accounts, identifying provider-side information associated with a person or request may be limited.

8. Legal bases and your choices

PlateScan processes meal submissions to provide the feature you request. Optional Firebase Analytics is enabled only with your consent, which you may refuse or withdraw without losing core functionality. Limited technical information may be processed where necessary for security, reliability, abuse prevention, and legal compliance.

9. Advertising and identifiers

PlateScan does not display third-party advertising, sell meal information, or use Firebase Analytics advertising-ID support in its iOS app. Analytics is not used unless you opt in. Firebase may nevertheless use persistent app or device identifiers, so Analytics information is not necessarily anonymous.

10. Security

PlateScan uses HTTPS, request-size limits, timeouts, rate limits, privacy-conscious diagnostics, and provider usage limits. No internet service or storage method is completely secure.

11. International processing

Those countries may have different data-protection laws. Where required, international processing must rely on an applicable legal mechanism and safeguards provided through the relevant service arrangement.
